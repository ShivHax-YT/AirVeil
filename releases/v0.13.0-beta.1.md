# AirVeil 0.13.0 beta 1

Adds a first-use notch tutorial and automatic camera-needed rounded edge lighting. Includes the idle-audio/device-handoff fix and first-launch Settings walkthrough from 0.12.0.

## Changes

- Four-step notch tour explains head tracking, centering, Face light, and controls. Back and Next navigate. Only **End tutorial** completes it; clicking elsewhere does not dismiss it. Sleep temporarily hides it, and an unfinished tour returns after relaunch.
- Tutorial illustrations do not start camera capture or illuminate the screen.
- Face light turns on after two distinct consecutive frames confirm a nearby face is too dark for a reliable pose. A broad, softly glowing rounded rectangular frame surrounds the built-in display with a transparent center.
- Manual Off prevents automatic relighting during the current check. Success, cancellation, timeout, missing/stale face evidence, and session inactivity switch it off. Display brightness settings stay unchanged.
- Native notch cleanup is safe when called repeatedly.

## Install

Download **AirVeil-0.13.0-apple-silicon.dmg**, quit AirVeil, and drag the app into Applications. The notch tutorial appears the first time the notch opens, including through **Show Notch Controls**.

Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later, and compatible head-tracking AirPods are required. This is a development-signed, non-notarized prerelease; another Mac may block first launch. Repository access is required while private.

## Validation

Full core regression suite passed. Native checks passed: 18 screen geometry assertions and 43 notch lifecycle assertions. Rendered 27 notch fixtures plus two edge-light sizes without camera capture or physical illumination. Traversed all four tutorial steps in the installed app. Verified the DMG read-only and confirmed its signed executable matches installed version 0.13.0 build 17.
