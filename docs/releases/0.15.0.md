# AirVeil 0.15.0 — prerelease

AirVeil now checks the foreground seat after sustained loss of an established AirPods motion stream. With camera assistance and seated dimming enabled, it dims the built-in display while you remain seated and restores the previous brightness when motion returns.

## Changes

- A permission walkthrough explains Camera, Screen Recording, and Head Tracking before the interactive tour. Each permission is optional, and the app verifies the actual macOS result.
- Permission cards use white Liquid Glass on macOS 26 or later, slower transitions, and blurred, dimmed background cards over subtle white stars and occasional short meteor streaks. Older systems use translucent material. Accessibility settings can reduce motion and transparency.
- Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Cookies & Local Storage open offline from the app footer.
- The both-AirPods workflow replaces independent earbud detection. The app no longer reads private per-ear Bluetooth metadata.
- Release builds exclude the developer-only **Preview Notch Animation** command.
- Settings remains a normal window in the Dock and Command-Tab; notch guidance appears above ordinary app windows.

## Validation and limits

The live wearer test confirmed seated dimming at the saved 2% target without locking, brightness restoration on return, and automatic return-camera attempts. Automated checks cover motion interruptions, camera cancellation, brightness ownership and recovery, presence decisions, permission choices, tutorial interaction, and native rendering. See the repository's validation records for the scope of each check.

Public headphone motion cannot prove that both earbuds are out. A sustained connection interruption can produce the same signal. A lost or discontinuous motion reference pauses tracking; use **Refresh direction** if automatic alignment cannot complete. Physical departure sleep/lock, 0% dimming, and extended-darkness acceptance remain separate from the completed 2% seated test.

When automatic display management is enabled but camera assistance or seated dimming is off, sustained motion loss requests display sleep directly. Password-on-wake follows macOS Lock Screen settings. AirVeil never unlocks the Mac.

## Install

Requires Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later, and compatible head-tracking AirPods. Drag AirVeil from the DMG to Applications, then open the app normally from Applications. This prerelease uses a persistent local development signature and is **not Apple-notarized**.

The installer contains the app, an Applications shortcut, and installation notes. It excludes local preferences, diagnostic files, and signing secrets. A SHA-256 checksum accompanies the DMG.
