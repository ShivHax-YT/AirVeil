# AirVeil 0.15.0 — release candidate

This candidate adds permission setup, repairs tutorial controls, and updates the both-AirPods display workflow. These notes describe the current source, not a published release.

## Changes

- **Permission setup:** Camera, Screen Recording, and Head Tracking cards precede the tutorial. Read to the end before allowing access, or decline immediately. AirVeil verifies the macOS result, saves incomplete progress, and offers retry and Settings actions.
- **Translucent cards:** White Liquid Glass on macOS 26 or later, translucent material on older systems, slower transitions, and blurred, dimmed side cards with aligned bottoms. Stars fade on and off faster; meteor streaks start after about one second and repeat every 4.5 seconds. Reduce Motion keeps the background still; Reduce Transparency uses solid cards.
- **Tutorial controls:** Highlighted controls and scrolling work throughout the 16-step tour, including dependent settings. The demo slider and Center affect only the example image. Head tracking has an explicit start action.
- **Offline policies:** Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Cookies & Local Storage open from the app footer before permission requests and afterward in Settings.
- **Removal and return:** Separate Power switches control **Lock when I leave** (on by default) and **Dim while I stay seated** (off until enabled). Both require camera assistance and a center check. Missing camera setup never falls back to immediate sleep. Returning motion restores owned brightness before direction recovery. Private per-ear metadata and Bluetooth permission are no longer used.
- **Low-light monitoring:** An explanatory notch animation appears before restoring brightness, then holds **Brightness restored** briefly and changes to a seat-check visual. Monitoring continues without dimming again during that removal episode. Darkness never proves departure.
- **Settings and head sync:** Preview, Tracking, Displays, Appearance, and Power tabs organize the controls. Sync head runs an explicit alignment, then the illustration, arc marker, and degree display follow real turns without changing saved blur thresholds.
- **Wear reminder:** The animated AirPods reminder includes × to dismiss only the presentation and **Turn off feature** to restore brightness and pause background checks. Enable blur while AirPods are out waits for their return and starts blur after successful camera alignment.
- **Windows and notch:** Settings appears in the Dock and Command-Tab as a normal window. Notch guidance stays above ordinary app windows. Its black backing expands on both sides and downward around the hardware notch; camera preview and face-check glyph behavior are unchanged.
- **Release configuration:** The developer-only **Preview Notch Animation** command is excluded from release builds and the packaged DMG.

## Validation and limits

Build 28 passes the full regression suite and native Settings, tour, notch, and policy-reader checks, and is installed with verified signature/package identity. New low-light and dismiss-button physical acceptance remains pending. Earlier wearer tests accepted the Settings wait → alignment → automatic-blur route and live head/arc/readout behavior. Build 27 passed four brightness recovery cases: both AirPods return orders after manual lock and actual Apple-menu Sleep. See the [current goal checklist](../../validation/GOAL-CHECKLIST-0.15.0-28.md) and [physical brightness record](../../validation/SETTINGS-WEAR-AND-WAKE-0.15.0-24.md). Offscreen renders do not establish all live animation behavior.

Public headphone motion is a removal proxy: it cannot distinguish both earbuds being out from sustained wireless loss or identify individual ears. Invalid tracking pauses blur; use **Refresh direction** if automatic alignment cannot complete. Automatic departure sleep in the current dark room, repeated hardware cycles, and the direct notch Enable route remain separate acceptance items.

Only confirmed absence can request enabled display sleep. After low-light brightness restoration, fresh dark frames keep monitoring without guessing departure; stale frames, framing loss, or camera failure retain bounded cleanup. Password-on-wake follows macOS Lock Screen settings. AirVeil never unlocks the Mac.

## Install

Requires Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later, and compatible head-tracking AirPods. Drag AirVeil from the DMG to Applications, then open it normally from Applications. This candidate uses a persistent local development signature and is **not Apple-notarized**.

Local packaging produces a DMG and SHA-256 checksum. The installer contains the app, bundled policies, an Applications shortcut, and installation notes; it excludes local preferences, diagnostic files, and signing secrets. Packaging does not publish a release.
