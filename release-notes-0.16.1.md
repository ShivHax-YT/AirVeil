# AirVeil 0.16.1 — build 36

Final release of the current feature set, including all merged AirVeil worktrees and subsequent native tab haptic fixes.

- Native Liquid Glass settings, smoother permissions transitions, and visibility-aware stars and meteors.
- Trackpad feedback for buttons, switches, selections, sliders, and the onset dial.
- Top tabs now request feedback on press and while dragging across tab boundaries, including pressing the selected tab. Native glass tracking remains intact; programmatic navigation stays silent.
- Low-light face-search fallback offers the notch Face light button even before detecting a face.
- Includes the settings/window sizing, onboarding, notch, and brightness-recovery fixes.

Download the Apple silicon DMG, quit AirVeil, and drag the app into Applications. Existing preferences are retained. Requires macOS 14 or later and compatible AirPods for head tracking.

Development-signed, not Apple-notarized; another Mac may block first launch. Hardware behavior depends on the Mac, AirPods, lighting, and system settings. Automated tests verify software behavior and rendering, not physical trackpad sensation or every hardware scenario. Processing stays local.
