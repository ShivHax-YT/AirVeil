### Display-management distinction checked against the current code

`AppModel.usesRemovalPresence` requires both seated dimming and camera assistance. With automatic display management enabled and a debounced motion-loss event, that path starts `RemovalPresenceCoordinator` with `allowUncertainSleep: false`. A usable seat reference allows present → dim and confirmed absent → sleep; unavailable/unknown does not cause sleep. When either seated dimming or camera assistance is off, the same enabled automatic display management instead requests display sleep directly, without a camera check. Disabling camera alone therefore does not disable display sleep. The Terms and Privacy Policy state this distinction. Product settings must also explain it near the controls. The command requests display sleep; password-on-wake remains macOS policy.

## Primary-source findings and application

### Clear notices and truthful claims

The FTC directs app developers to make data practices and choices clear, honor privacy/security promises, and disclose significant changes rather than relying only on revised text. **Application:** avoid absolute claims such as “100% private,” “no personal data,” “nothing stored,” “encrypted data,” or “guaranteed protection” when the code does not support them. Provide a short summary with the full notice and explain optional camera behavior near enablement. [FTC, Marketing Your Mobile App](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/marketing-your-mobile-app-get-it-right-start)

California's CalOPPA specifies policy content for covered commercial sites/online services, including information categories, third-party categories, effective date, change notification, and relevant tracking disclosures. Its application to this operator and distribution model remains to be determined. **Application:** include those factual categories without asserting that the operator is necessarily subject to CalOPPA or CCPA. [California Business and Professions Code §22575](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=22575.)

For covered UK processing, the ICO's notice guidance identifies operator/contact, purposes, lawful basis, recipients/transfers, retention, rights, and relevant automated decisions among required information. **Application:** an invented operator/contact or blanket “consent” basis would make the draft misleading. Camera permission is a technical access choice, not a complete jurisdictional analysis. [ICO, Right to be informed](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/individual-rights/right-to-be-informed/)

### Nevada scope and requests

Nevada's operator definition requires commercial website/online-service operation, covered-information collection/maintenance, and a Nevada nexus; being an individual is not an automatic exclusion. Section 603A.340 specifies notice content; its exception requires all stated conditions, including revenue source and fewer than 20,000 annual unique visitors. No exception is assumed here. Sections 603A.325/.345 allow an email request address and require covered operators to handle verified sale opt-outs, ordinarily within 60 days, with a qualified 30-day extension. **Application:** the notice designates the supplied email and states no sale. Applicability to this local app and separate support/distribution remains fact-dependent. [Nevada NRS 603A.330–.345](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-603a.html#NRS603ASec330)

### Cookies, native storage, and consent

The ICO expressly explains that device-storage/access rules are technology-neutral and can apply to apps and connected devices, not just cookies or personal information. **Application:** the absence of browser cookies is a factual disclosure, not an exemption from every storage/access rule. [ICO, What are storage and access technologies?](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/what-are-storage-and-access-technologies/)

The ICO finalized updated guidance on April 29, 2026. Its exceptions depend on purpose and conditions. “Strictly necessary” requires essential delivery of a requested service, not convenience to the provider; the UK appearance exception has transparency and easy-objection conditions. **Application:** do not classify all persistent preferences as essential. Current Reset defaults is not a general session-only or storage-objection control. Assess each purpose and market; the UK appearance exception must not be assumed to apply across the EU. [ICO, What are the exceptions?](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/what-are-the-exceptions/), [ICO guidance update](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/)

The EDPB's final October 16, 2024 technical-scope guidelines address Article 5(3), including processing on terminal equipment. They do not establish a blanket exemption for local processing. **Application:** separate technical scope, national implementation, and purpose-specific exceptions; do not infer automatic consent compliance from being offline. [EDPB Guidelines 2/2023, final version](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/guideline/guidelines-22023-on-technical-scope-of-art-53-of-eprivacy-directive_en)

### Apple and external services

Apple's App Store privacy-label instructions use a particular off-device definition of data collection and say on-device-only processing is not collected for those answers. **Application:** this is potentially useful for a future App Store submission, not a statement that the app processes no personal information under other laws. No present App Store approval or notarization is established by this audit. [Apple, App privacy details](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/)

Apple explains that screen-recording access is controlled in Privacy & Security. **Application:** show accurate permission controls, and distinguish the OS permission name from this app's explicit audio-disabled configuration. [Apple, Screen and audio recording permissions](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/mac-help/mchl592e5686/mac)

GitHub's own privacy and cookie notices cover its website/account/service handling. **Application:** app-local no-network claims must not be extended to download pages, repository visits, or user-posted issues. No separate AirVeil website or support intake service was verified. [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), [GitHub Cookies](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-cookies)

The supplied contact uses Gmail. Google's notice describes processing of information and content through its services. **Application:** disclose the separate email-provider relationship rather than claiming support mail remains only on a user's Mac. The developer's own retention and request handling must also follow the published notice; a provider policy does not determine those commitments. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
